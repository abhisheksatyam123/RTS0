## Differences

1. **Units in a formation get armour, damage, attack-rate and speed changes that 0.28 doesn't have.**
   - 0.28: no formation template changes a member's stats. template_formation.xml and every special/formations/*.xml hold only Formation, FormationAttack, Identity, Obstruction, Position, UnitAI and UnitMotion (phalanx.xml:1-28 for example). None has Auras or modifiers, and no file in simulation/data/auras is of type "formation" (Auras.js:154-177 HasFormationAura would read that). The only speed effect is Formation.js ComputeMotionParameters: the slowest member's WalkSpeed times the template's SpeedMultiplier (Formation.js:899-932), which is 1 for every land formation (template_formation.xml SpeedMultiplier).
   - RTS0: `formation_stat_modifiers` (src/simulation/formation.h:67-114, added as todo T22 "matching 0 A.D. templates") gives each formation invented values. Box gives +1 hack, pierce and crush and x0.9 speed. Phalanx gives +5/+5/+2, x1.2 damage and x0.75 speed. Testudo gives +3/+10/+1 and x0.6 speed. Wedge gives -2 armour, x1.3 damage, x0.85 repeat time and x1.1 speed. Forced March gives -2 armour and x1.2 speed. There are more rows. They apply in `unit_base_walk_speed` (src/simulation/simulation.cpp:12005-12008), `unit_attack_effects` (:18603-18609), `unit_repeat_time` (:18617-18622) and `unit_resistance` (:19862-19867). Box is the default formation for every walk (gui.session.defaultformation), and units stay in it once they stop. So nearly every army a player has walked fights with +1 armour and marches 10% slower than in 0.28. The formation's own speed, `min(unit_base_walk_speed) * t.speed_multiplier` (:8619-8640), includes the multiplier as well. tools/test_formations.cpp:842 (section 16) asserts these values.
   - High. Fix: delete `FormationStatMods` and `formation_stat_modifiers` and the four call sites. Replace test section 16 with a check that joining a formation leaves resistance, damage, repeat time and walk speed unchanged. Mark T22 in todo.md as reverted.

2. **A Shift-queued walk or patrol puts loose units into the default formation at once.**
   - 0.28: ProcessCommand passes a command's formation only when the order isn't queued, or when it is the null formation (helpers/Commands.js:898-902). A queued "walk" or "patrol" (unit_actions.js:54-64, :314 use `g_AutoFormation.getDefault()`) therefore reaches GetFormationUnitAIs with no template. Loose units have the null formation there (:1617) and each queue an individual walk. When all of them are individuals, the walk spreads them with DistributeAround (:104-115). Their current orders run first. Only units already in a formation walk in it, and the order is queued on their controller.
   - RTS0: `post_lockstep` calls `apply_default_formation` for every NET_CMD_MOVE and NET_CMD_PATROL, queued or not (src/presentation/main_node.cpp:1609-1610). The local paths do the same (session_input.cpp:996, :1162; session_pointer.cpp:703, :711). `apply_default_formation` (main_node.cpp:792-809) sends an unqueued NET_CMD_FORMATION, and turn_manager.cpp:1763-1764 runs it as `order_set_formation`. That forms the units up at once, and `formation_move_members_into(ci, true, true)` replaces each member's task (simulation.cpp:7795-7812: target_resource_id cleared, ACTION_MOVE). `split_queued` then puts the queued walk on the new controller's queue (:20264-20281). The controller is idle, so `tick_formations` starts the walk on the next tick (:8427-8440). A Shift-click with loose gatherers or builders selected pulls them off their work into a Box that marches straight away. In 0.28 they finish their work and then walk on their own.
   - High. Fix: in `apply_default_formation`, take the queued flag and return when it is set. Pass `param & NET_PARAM_QUEUED` from post_lockstep, and `queue_shift`/`queued` from the local calls.

3. **Selecting units from two different formations (or loose units with them) breaks them all up on a walk instead of putting them into the default formation.**
   - 0.28: GetFormationUnitAIs keeps the template the GUI sent (the default, e.g. Box) unless every selected formation shares one (helpers/Commands.js:1589-1601: `if (template && !forceTemplate) formationTemplate = template`). Units from Box and Close Order, with or without loose units, are therefore all formed into new default-formation controllers (:1604-1671).
   - RTS0: `apply_default_formation` returns as soon as any selected unit is in a formation (main_node.cpp:796-798). The walk then reaches `formation_controllers_for` with no template (`want` UNDEFINED, simulation.cpp:7895-7900 keeps it UNDEFINED when the templates differ). Each cluster is taken out of its formation and, with `want == UNDEFINED`, ordered as individuals (:7957-7962). Both formations disband and everyone walks loose. With the default formation off, 0.28 does the same, so the two only differ when a default is set. Box is the default.
   - Medium. Fix: make the walk carry the default formation the way 0.28's command does. Add a template parameter to `cmd_move`/`order_walk`/`order_patrol` (a NET_CMD_MOVE/PATROL field, FORMATION_NONE meaning "undefined"). Pass it as `tmpl` with `force_template=false` to `formation_controllers_for`. Use it where `want` would stay UNDEFINED: no units in a formation, or several formations with different templates. Then drop the separate NET_CMD_FORMATION from `apply_default_formation`, which also fixes item 2 once the queued case sends no template.

4. **Queued null-formation orders take units out of their formation at once.**
   - 0.28: with gui.session.formationwalkonly (default true), every order but walk and patrol sends the null formation (AutoFormation.js:62-66; unit_actions.js:115, :161, :213, :365, :432, :491, :543, :828, :902, :956; input.js:338, :381), and it passes even when queued (Commands.js:901). GetFormationUnitAIs calls `LeaveFormation(cmd.queued || false)` (:1617-1622). A queued LeaveFormation is added behind the member's current order (UnitAI.js:5209-5220), and ReplaceOrder keeps everything from a LeaveFormation on when the controller re-issues FormationWalk (:4140-4152, "needed to support queued no-formation orders"). A marching formation given a Shift-queued gather, attack or garrison therefore finishes its march in shape. Each member then leaves and does the queued task.
   - RTS0: `post_lockstep` sends NET_CMD_FORMATION with FORMATION_NONE ahead of the order whatever its queued bit (main_node.cpp:1611-1627). `order_set_formation(..., FORMATION_NONE)` removes the units at once (simulation.cpp:8003-8006). The line drag does the same (session_input.cpp:1797-1808), as 0.28's walk-custom also sends NULL_FORMATION (input.js:1274). The formation breaks the moment Shift is clicked. Each member walks to the slot it last had and then starts the queued task.
   - Medium. Fix: when the order is queued, don't send the leave command. Instead queue a "leave formation" order behind each member's current order (a new ORDER_LEAVE_FORMATION handled in finish_order, calling `formation_remove_units` on that unit), and have the controller's queue keep it. Or, more simply, append the queued order to the controller's queue with a flag that disbands the formation when the controller reaches it.

5. **Leaving with the null formation re-forms the rest of a marching formation.**
   - 0.28: Order.LeaveFormation wraps RemoveMembers in `SetRearrange(false)` … `SetRearrange(true)` (UnitAI.js:294-307), so the members that stay keep their slots. Only a lone selected unit (RemoveFromFormation, Commands.js:1543-1553) and deaths go through RemoveMembers with the controller's own rearrange flag (Formation.js:423-429).
   - RTS0: the null formation goes through `formation_remove_units` (simulation.cpp:8003-8006, and the `leaving` list at :7913-7919). That re-forms a walking, approaching or patrolling formation (:7700). Picking part of a marching formation (with selection.singleselection or the idle-unit keys) and sending it to gather recentres and reshapes the rest of the formation mid-march.
   - Low. Fix: give `formation_remove_units` a `rearrange` argument (default true). Pass false from `order_set_formation`'s FORMATION_NONE branch when count > 1, and from the `leaving` path in `formation_controllers_for`.

6. **Changing a whole formation to a template some members can't take drops those members; 0.28 keeps them.**
   - 0.28: with the whole formation selected and a different template forced, GetFormationUnitAIs calls `cmpFormation.LoadFormation(template)` (Commands.js:1580-1581). That is ChangeEntityTemplate (Formation.js:1044-1048, Transform.js:4-160, which copies the orders), and OnEntityRenamed then calls `SetMembers(members)` with every old member (Formation.js:1051-1056). SetMembers doesn't check CanUseFormation (:356-380). Members that can't use the new template are only kept out of `formedUnits` (Commands.js:1617-1624). Their MoveIntoFormation does nothing for a non-controller (UnitAI.js:5249-5260), and they stay in the new formation. For example, 12 hoplites and 2 archers in a Box switched to Phalanx stay one 14-unit Phalanx.
   - RTS0: the LoadFormation branch removes every member that can't use the new template (simulation.cpp:7871-7891, "Members that cannot use it leave"). In the example, the two archers drop out and stand loose.
   - Low. Fix: keep every member in that branch (drop the `leave` list). `individual` stays empty because the controller still holds them.

7. **Members don't turn to the formation's heading when they reach their slots.**
   - 0.28: a member whose FormationWalk finishes runs FinishOrder. With its queue empty, that calls `SetFinishedEntity` (UnitAI.js:3945-3958), which turns the member to the controller's rotation (`cmpEntPosition.TurnTo(cmpPosition.GetRotation().y)`, Formation.js:297-305). A formation that forms up or arrives ends with every member facing the formation's front.
   - RTS0: nothing sets a member's heading at its slot. On arrival `tick_formations` only clears `following` (simulation.cpp:8707-8722). A member keeps the heading of its last step (move_units sets heading from the step, :13607-13608), and forming up in place sends members along paths with no facing (:7812-7830). A block formed from units coming from several sides stands facing every way.
   - Low-medium (visible on every formation). Fix: when a member with `formation_group_` set goes idle from a formation walk (the `arrived[i]` branch at :12820, or when `tick_formations` clears `following` on arrival), set its heading to the controller's `fc.rot` (or let it turn there at its TurnRate, as Position.TurnTo does).

8. **The idle re-form packs an unpacked siege engine and walks it back to its slot.**
   - 0.28: Order.FormationWalk on a packable unit finishes at once when the controller is idle, "as that would unpack us" (UnitAI.js:263-279). The IDLE timer's MoveMembersIntoFormation (:1019-1025) therefore leaves an unpacked catapult or bolt shooter where it stands.
   - RTS0: the IDLE timer's `formation_move_members_into(ci, false, false)` (simulation.cpp:8587) gives every drifted member `has_order` and ACTION_MOVE (:7805-7808). `tick_pack` packs an unpacked engine that has an order (:16639-16647). An engine that chased something and unpacked to fire packs again and rolls back into line.
   - Low. Fix: in `formation_move_members_into`, skip members whose `pack_state` isn't PACK_NONE or PACK_PACKED while `fc.order == FORDER_IDLE`.

9. **The Formation panel holds four buttons a row, not five.**
   - 0.28: the panel has rowLength 5 (selection_panels.js:256) and setPanelObjectPosition puts 38 px buttons 1 px apart (unit_commands.js:25-40; formation_panel.xml). Nine formations take two rows, and the maximum of 15 takes three.
   - RTS0: `actions_bar_` is an HFlowContainer 194 px wide with 2 px separation (src/presentation/command_queue_view.h:235-241; kLeftW 206 at :2480). Five buttons need 198 px, so it wraps after four. Nine formations take three rows, and 15 take four, which is 158 px in a 116 px box.
   - Nit. Fix: set h_separation and v_separation to 1 (5 x 38 + 4 = 194 fits).
