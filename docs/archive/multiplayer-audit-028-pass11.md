## Checked, matches 0.28

- **Disconnect texts in the setup and the join flow.** The timeout box leaves out "Please ensure that you entered the correct server name or IP address, and port number." while signed in to the lobby (`mn:986-994`, as `gui/common/network.js:126-131`). Apart from gap 1.
- **Setup tooltips and controls.** Back's "Return to the lobby." or "Return to the main menu." (`gs:2458`, as `CancelButton.js`). The AI configuration opens read-only for clients (`gs:1697-1702`, as `AIConfigButton.js` and `AIConfigPage.openPage`). Picking AI, Unassigned or Removed on a client's slot makes that client an observer (`gs:2843`, as `PlayerAssignmentItem.AI.onSelectionChange`).
- **Joiners and AI slots.** A joiner is seated in place of an AI when no free human slot is left, so a 1v1 against an AI fills with the joiner (`mn:3158-3168`, as `findAssignmentSlot` and `PlayerAssignment.onPlayerAssignmentsChange` removing the AI).
- **Sound notifications.** The `nick` chime in the setup and the match, with its 3 s threshold, and `gamesetup.join` off by default (`sl:1618-1623`, `mn:3359-3362`, as `gui/common/functions_utility.js:7`, `:200-213` and `SoundNotification.js`).
- **Session menu in multiplayer.** Save stays available (`scenes/session/pause_menu.gd:177-180`, as `MenuButtons.js:49-71`). Time warp is off in networked games (`mn:1952-1953`, as `TimeWarp.js:29-30`).
- **Lobby listing of a finished game.** The host's game is unregistered once the match scene is gone, which is when 0.28's `endGame` calls `SendUnregisterGame` (`scenes/menu/lobby_session.gd:489-500`, as `gui/session/session.js:535-536`).
- **Names and limits.** `playername.multiplayer` is saved on host and join (`scenes/menu/gamesetup_mp.gd:284-289`, as `gamesetup_mp.js:421`, `:485`). The 41-client cap with "Server full." (`src/network/net_types.h:299-314`, as `NS:78`, `NS:1049`, `NS:1113-1118`).
