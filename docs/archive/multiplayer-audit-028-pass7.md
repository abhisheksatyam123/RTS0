# RTS0 multiplayer vs 0 A.D. 0.28: seventh pass (who counts as an observer, setup readiness, rejoin after the end, hosting errors)

Read-only code audit, 2026-10-20. RTS0 is at `main` 7978cd951 (after #1182, which closed pass 6). This pass leaves out everything that `audit-028.md`, passes 2 to 5 (`/mnt/project-files/multiplayer/`) and pass 6 (`docs/archive/multiplayer-audit-028-pass6.md`) list as fixed, skipped on purpose, or still open in their tables. Nothing was built or run. I checked each gap below in both codebases by reading the code, and grepped RTS0 for the 0.28 behaviour.

Most gaps come from one cause. 0.28 decides whether "I am an observer" from the client's own seat: `g_IsObserver = isPlayerObserver(Engine.GetPlayerID())`, which is true with no seat, or once the seat has won or lost (`gui/session/top_panel/PlayerViewControl.js:97`, `gui/session/session.js:456-460`). Picking a player in the viewPlayer dropdown changes only `g_ViewedPlayer`. RTS0 asks `local_player()` in many places, and that returns the **viewed** player for anyone who spectates (`src/presentation/main_node.h:500-504`). It also treats a defeated or victorious seat as a player.

## Checked, matches 0.28

- **Pause relay and rejoin.** The host stamps the sender, relays to everyone else, drops a leaver's pause, and tells a rejoiner who is pausing (`tm:2020-2034`, `tm:620-629`, as `NS:1531-1565`). "Paused by" names each pauser in its colour, and "Click to resume" shows only for your own pause (`mn:766-797`, as `PauseOverlay.js:29-45`). There is no per-player pause limit, as in 0.28.
- **Observer view picker.** A loss switches the view to Observer, and a win keeps your own view (`mn:1321-1352`, as `PlayerViewControl.js:56-71`). A defeated rejoiner starts on Observer, as `onPlayersInit` does. Follow Player is shown only while a player is picked.
- **Won/lost box for the host.** The host is not invited to quit while other humans still play (`ma:344-351`, as `QuitConfirmationDefeat.js:36-40`).
- **Flares.** The 3 s send cooldown, the 16-in-6-s receive limit, `gui.session.notifications.flare` "never"/"observer"/"always", and "has sent a flare." with the focus tooltip (`sl:98-135`, as `messages.js:507-526`, `ChatMessageFormatSimulation.js:178-206`). Their observer test is the one in gap 1.
- **Network commands.** `/kick`, `/ban`, `/kickspecs`, `/banspecs`, `/list`, `/clear`, with "Only the host can kick clients!" and both "Offline game!" texts (`mn:336-376`, as `network.js:162-198`).
- **Delay overlay and status overlay.** "Delay to live stream" from 6 turns at 5 turns a second (`sl:1531-1550`). The disconnect reasons and the "Connection Error" box with Open FAQ (`mn:884-930`). "Waiting for players to connect:" and "Synchronizing gameplay…" (`mn:938-961`).
- **Defaults.** Every `[network]` key in 0.28 `default.cfg` (lateobservers everyone, observerlimit 8, observermaxlag -1, autocatchup, duplicateplayernames) has the same default (`src/presentation/user_config.cpp:96-100`).
- **Prelobby.** Username, password and confirmation checks with 0.28's texts (`scenes/menu/lobby_session.gd:205-215`). "Are you sure you want to delete the password after connecting?" (`scenes/menu/lobby.gd:691`). Terms acceptance before sign-in and registration (`lobby.gd:775-835`). Registration signs in afterwards, as register.js + entrance.js do.
- **Lobby lists.** The game-rating average with the default rating for unrated players and observers left out (`lobby_session.gd:579-588`, as `Game.js:173-209`). The rating filter values, map size, map type, player count and open-games filters (`lobby.gd:1135-1159`). Player-list sorting. The leaderboard Update button.
- **Replays and summary.** Multiplayer and rated classification (`scenes/menu/replay_browser.gd:174-175`, as `replay_menu.js:130-136`). The summary captions, including "You have been disconnected." (`scenes/session/summary_screen.gd:570-586`, as `summary.js:512-525`), apart from the observer case in gap 1. Watch Replay is hidden without a replay, as for rejoined clients in 0.28 (`session.js:537`).
- **Setup.** The Start button gate and its tooltips. Tab completion of names, civs and settings in the setup chat (`gs:2429-2489`). Rated Game and Cheats coupling (`gs:2350-2366`, `:2509-2530`).
- **Connect flow strings.** "Server is not running.", "Failed to get local IP of the server…" and "Connection failed" (`gamesetup_mp.js:217-237`) are not in RTS0. They belong to 0.28's XMPP connection-data path (`serverdata` messages), which RTS0 replaces with `POST /rooms/connect`, whose refusals have their own texts. Not listed.
