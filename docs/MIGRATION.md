# 0 A.D. code migration: status and todo

This file tracks the migration of the vendored 0 A.D. C++ in `src/0ad/` (reference only; `SConstruct` skips it) into live RTS0 code. Update it in every PR that ports, changes or drops a piece. Gameplay parity with 0 A.D.'s JS systems is tracked separately in `docs/REMAINING_GAPS.md` and `docs/PLAN.md`. 0 A.D. data imported by tools rather than ported code (assets, maps, unit rosters and heroes from `tools/extract_0ad_rosters.py`) is listed in `docs/0AD_ATTRIBUTION.md`.

Last updated: 2026-10-24 (networking turn management, late-join sync, grid/pathfinding helpers, and map structures ported).

Status key: **Done**, ported and in use · **Ported**, ported and tested but not used by the sim yet · **Partial** · **Todo** · **Not planned**

## Todo, in priority order

This list covers porting the vendored C++ only. Overall project priorities and gameplay-parity work are in `docs/PLAN.md` and `docs/NEXT_STEPS.md`; this list doesn't repeat them.

1. **Obstruction shapes.** Done (maps-path, 2026-10-16). Rasterization is done, and building footprints are rectangles that turn with the building (`nav/footprint.h`, the axes of `CCmpObstruction::GetObstructionSquare`) in the nav grid, short paths, `separation()`, gates and reach checks. A building has a half width (`half_size`) and a half depth (`half_d`, from `building_half_depth`, equal for every building so far), and a quarter turn swaps them (`Building::hu`, `hv`). 0 A.D.'s `CCmpObstructionManager` isn't vendored, and RTS0 doesn't need a full one: `buildings_` and the `UnitGrid` in `separation()` give VertexPathfinder its shapes. Placement follows `CCmpObstruction::CheckFoundation`: every building marks the grid cells its turned footprint reaches into (`occupied_cells_`, FULL where it covers a cell, PART where it only reaches in), so a square of whole cells is blocked wherever a footprint overlaps it, and a turned foundation is tested shape against shape where it only shares PART cells (`CCmpObstructionManager::TestStaticShape`, `nav::footprints_overlap`). A destroyed or deleted structure no longer blocks its ground (a 0 A.D. corpse has no Obstruction). Units keep their 0 A.D. passability class Clearance from buildings (`unit_building_clearance`: 0.8 m, 3 m for the "large" class of siege, elephants, chariots and catafalques, which `unit_is_large` reads from the roster row's classes), and so do animals (`fauna_can_step`, large for the bull and the adult elephants); terrain (deep water, cliffs) is still tested by the navcell alone, since RTS0's 8 m navcells are wider than either clearance. Between units, RTS0 keeps its own `unit_collision_radius`, where 0 A.D. gives the obstruction the clearance too. Range checks against structures (attack, capture, build and repair, dropsite returns, trade arrival) measure IsInTargetRange's gap between the unit's circle and the turned footprint (`structure_gap`), not the distance to the centre. Determinism: `GlobalRegionIds` stays flat per-chunk arrays in vendor `Recompute` order; no new `unordered_map` iteration (edge work uses indexed loops only). Verified: `check_parity --unit nav-live` PASS 12/12, `test_long_pathfinder` PASS (8027 reachable, avg 53.4 us, 121 pops/query), `test_vertex_pathfinder` PASS.
2. **Unit pushing.** Done. `separation()` ports `CCmpUnitMotionManager`'s pushing (`CCmpUnitMotion_System.cpp` `Push`, `pathfinder.xml` `<Pushing>`): moving units push moving units and idle units push idle units, never one another, and a walker whose body still overlaps an idle unit's (round a crowded goal) is nudged round it, sideways and never back, while the idle unit stays where it is; a pair pushes within its clearances times Radius plus the moving or static extension, at a force that peaks at Spread of that reach and is capped by `MAX_DISTANCE_FACTOR`; the heavier unit pushes harder (UnitMotion Weight: 10, 50 for siege and elephants); units that have walked through each other get the sideways nudge; and a unit's total push under MinimalForce per turn is dropped, so crowds settle. A walking crowd closes up as in 0 A.D.: its rear carries less pressure and so walks faster, and the pushes between walkers mostly cancel under MinimalForce until a pair comes within half its MovingSpread distance (0.96 units for two swordsmen), so `test_unit_pushing` checks that distance on the way, not the bodies' width. A formation's members don't push each other while they hold their slots. Animals push and are pushed in the same pass (#1528), as 0.28's manager holds every entity with UnitMotion: their passability clearance (a bull or an adult elephant pushes as a war elephant) and UnitMotion Weight set the push, their pressure slows them (`fauna_move_speed`), and animals that don't block movement stay out. An animal and the entity it bites, or the bystanders round an animal standing to bite, don't push each other while they stand, since 0.28 measures melee reach edge to edge and a static push never takes a biter out of reach there; at RTS0's crowd scale one would. Units standing still obstruct a walker (`avoid_idle_units`, `CheckMovement`'s `ControlGroupMovementObstructionFilter`), which stops and takes a short path round them (`search_short_path_with_units`, `HandleObstructedMove`'s range of 12 m plus 4 m per failed turn, at 4 m a unit). RTS0 keeps only the path's next waypoint, so a walker that reaches one searches on from there, 32 m round, instead of heading back to where it was stuck. The path leads to the task's range round its target once that is in the search range, shaped as `ComputeGoal` shapes it (a circle round a unit or resource, a square round a structure), and a walker already within reach of its task stays where it is. Idle units' pushes can leave a walker in a slit between two of their squares, narrower than the search's corner offset, where no search finds a way; one whose task (a relic it captures among them) is within a step along an axis slides there. RTS0 keeps `unit_collision_radius` for bodies and gives pushing 0 A.D.'s clearances scaled to it (`unit_push_clearance`, `PUSH_SCALE`), so two villagers still settle 1.4 units apart. Pushing pressure (`MotionState::pushingPressure`, kept in `MotionTurn`'s spare byte, so the save format doesn't change) builds up once per turn with each pair that pushes, decays by PressureDecay, dampens a unit's pushes and slows its walk to no less than 1.5 m/s (`PerformMove`, `unit_move_speed`). Ship passengers come ashore on their own free spots (`ship_unload_point`), and siege tower and ram passengers on free spots round the engine's square footprint (`siege_spawn_point`), both on the `Footprint.PickSpawnPoint` rules that #264 ported for trained and ungarrisoned units. A passenger with no free spot stays aboard, or drowns when its ship sinks (`EjectOrKill`); a siege engine's forced eject (EjectHealth, or the engine's destruction) puts it at the engine's centre, as `GetSpawnPosition` does when forced.
3. **JumpPointCache.** Not needed yet. Maps now reach 512, but on 2026-10-08 JPS long paths were only about 3% of a 20-minute Mainland match on a 512 map (callgrind). Re-checked 2026-10-16 (maps-path): `test_long_pathfinder` avg 53.4 us, 121 pops/query; `check_parity --unit nav-live` 12/12 optimal and deterministic. No new evidence to revisit; full 1 m grid stays too invasive. Revisit if that share grows.
4. **Fixed-point simulation (optional).** Whether the sim moves from `float` to `rtso::Fixed` is a multiplayer-model decision. Cross-platform lockstep doesn't need it: G30 makes the float simulation compute the same bits on every build (contraction off, one RNG draw order, platform-independent transcendental functions in `det_math.h`). See `docs/PLAN.md` P5 and `docs/MULTIPLAYER.md`. `src/core/math/fixed.h` is ready if that decision is made. It would also need `CFixedVector2D`, which isn't vendored.
